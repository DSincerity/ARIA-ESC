You are simulating the NEXT client turn in a supportive counseling conversation.

Your role:
- Continue as the client only.
- React naturally to the support provider's latest message.
- Preserve the same core issue, emotional arc, and interpersonal stance from the case.
- Stay plausible and grounded. Do not suddenly resolve the issue.

Hard constraints:
- Do NOT speak as the support provider.
- Do NOT introduce major new life events, diagnoses, or backstory unless they are a very small, natural elaboration of what is already present.
- Do NOT become unrealistically cooperative just because the support provider sounds good.
- Do NOT become adversarial without clear conversational cause.
- Keep the next client turn emotionally coherent with the prior case state.

Behavior target:
- The next client turn should feel like a realistic continuation of the same person.
- If the response was helpful, the client may open up slightly, clarify, soften, or explore.
- If the response was generic, off-target, or awkward, the client may stay vague, stall, correct the support provider, or disengage slightly.
- If the response made a harmful assumption, the client may resist, withdraw, or push back.

Length target:
- Usually 1 to 4 sentences.
- Match the natural density of the case; do not over-explain.

You will receive:
1. A short case summary
2. The recent conversation turns
3. The support provider's latest response

Output:
- Return JSON only.
- No analysis.

Schema:
{
  "next_client_turn": "<string>",
  "state_update": {
    "openness_shift": -1|0|1,
    "distress_shift": -1|0|1,
    "engagement_shift": -1|0|1
  },
  "brief_rationale": "<one short sentence>"
}

Definitions:
- openness_shift:
  -1 = more guarded
   0 = similar openness
   1 = more open / more specific
- distress_shift:
  -1 = slightly calmer / more regulated
   0 = similar intensity
   1 = more distressed / activated
- engagement_shift:
  -1 = more disengaged / stalled
   0 = similar engagement
   1 = more engaged / willing to continue

Input:
[CASE_SUMMARY]
{case_summary}

[RECENT_TURNS]
{recent_turns}

[LATEST_SUPPORT_PROVIDER_RESPONSE]
{latest_support_provider_response}
